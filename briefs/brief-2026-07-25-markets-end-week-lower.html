The chipmaker rout, on the other hand, is a concern due to the importance of the technology sector in the overall economy. The technology sector accounts for a significant portion of the S&P 500, and a decline in this sector can have a ripple effect on the entire market.

The disappointing earnings season further added to the market's woes, as it suggests that companies may be struggling to maintain profitability in the current economic environment.
