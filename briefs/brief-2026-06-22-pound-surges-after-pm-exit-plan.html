What Happened

The UK Prime Minister's announcement to step down has triggered a significant reaction in the foreign exchange market, with the pound sterling experiencing a notable surge of over 1% against the US dollar and euro. This move is largely seen as a response to the political uncertainty that has been plaguing the UK, with investors welcoming the potential for a new leadership that could bring about more stability.

The pound's increase could also impact the UK's trade balance, as a stronger currency makes exports more expensive for foreign buyers, potentially leading to a decrease in export demand.
